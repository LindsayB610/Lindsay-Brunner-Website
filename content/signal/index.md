Signal is a private, owner-operated SEO and content-planning tool built by Lindsay Brunner. It turns search performance and keyword data into a prioritized list of editorial opportunities for [lindsaybrunner.com](/).

## What Signal does

Signal combines search performance from Google Search Console with historical keyword metrics from Google Ads. It uses that data to compare user-supplied keyword ideas, estimate search demand, and generate private SEO planning reports.

Signal is not an advertising platform. It does not create or modify campaigns, ads, billing, audiences, or account settings.

## How Signal uses Google Ads data

Signal requests the Google Ads OAuth scope to retrieve historical keyword metrics through the Google Ads API. Those metrics include average monthly searches, competition, and top-of-page bid ranges.

Google Ads data is used only to produce private SEO analysis for the authorized account. It is not sold, used to serve advertising, or exposed publicly.

## Access and support

Signal is not offered as a public software service. Access is limited to its owner and Google accounts explicitly authorized by the owner.

For details about data handling, read the [privacy policy](/privacy/) and [terms and conditions](/terms/). Questions about Signal can be sent to [lb@lindsaybrunner.com](mailto:lb@lindsaybrunner.com).
